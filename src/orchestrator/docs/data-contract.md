## Invariants

- A task with `status = completed` MUST have its `blocked_by` prerequisite also in `completed` status (or NULL)
- A task with `status = pruned` MUST NOT transition to any other status
- `depth` MUST NOT exceed 5; the scheduler enforces this before dispatch
- At most one `task_candidates` row per `task_id` may have `is_selected = 1`
- A `task_candidates` row MUST NOT be deleted after creation
- `projects.status` reflects the aggregate terminal state of all its tasks
